01 — your moment

Where Pesa is right now

You started as Pesapeer: one Nigerian in Canada, one brother who needed school fees, one transfer that cost too much and could vanish. That story became a licensed business across Canada, the US, the UK and Australia, serving 25,000+ people who move money between the countries they live in and the ones they love.

In March you opened your proprietary rails to other businesses. That is the real story: Pesa is no longer just an app you download — it is infrastructure other companies build on. Infrastructure is bought on trust, and trust is built by the humans you can see, not the API docs you can read.

I don't do promotions. I build outcomes.

I tell migration stories for a living — the money, the family, the quiet fear of being far away. My audience is largely Nigerian professionals and SME operators: exactly the people who send money home and the people who run businesses that need cross-border rails. I don't read a brand deck and write a caption. I sit with the story until it sounds like a person.

595 leads in days for Utiva (edtech). ~30,000 impressions on episode 1 of my podcast with high engagement. SME-community campaigns with SevenUp and Lagos Business School. TEDx organiser at 17 and 18. I optimise for the outcome, not the impression count.
