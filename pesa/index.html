01 · WHO YOU ARE TALKING TO

Your two audiences do not read the same things

One group sends money home. The other builds products on your rails. Your content has to speak to both without sounding like it is talking to neither.

01

The sender

Nigerian at home or abroad, 25 to 45, sending money to family every month. Cares about speed, rate, and whether it actually lands. Lives in WhatsApp groups and Instagram. Acts when someone they trust says the money arrived.

02

The business builder

Founder or ops lead at a small company that moves money across borders. Reads LinkedIn and newsletters. Cares about compliance, uptime and who is behind the rails. Acts after proof, not after ads.

03

Where they overlap

Both trust a person more than a logo. Both will watch a real transfer story before they sign up. That is the space I work in.
