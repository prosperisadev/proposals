Tolu,

I read the TechCabal piece in March about Pesa opening its rails to other businesses. I have been thinking about it since, because that is a different company from the app people download.

So this is not a media kit. It is one page about the story you now have to tell, and what I would do with it.

CHAPTER 01

Where you actually are

You are not selling transfers any more. You are selling trust to companies that will move other people's money through you.

01

The beginning is still your best asset

One Nigerian in Canada, one brother who needed school fees, one transfer that cost too much. People repeat that story at dinner. It is worth more than any ad you can buy.

02

March changed the category

Opening your rails to other businesses moved Pesa from app to infrastructure. People who buy infrastructure read, compare, and ask who is behind it. Nobody signs a contract with an API doc.

03

The person who decides is not on your app

The fintech founder, the shop owner, the operations lead. They do not scroll for a payments partner. They listen to someone they trust, usually on a podcast or in a group chat.

I am not an agency with a deck and a junior account manager. I am one person who writes, shoots, edits and publishes the work. My audience is Nigerian professionals and business owners, the same people who send money home and the same people who would put their business on your rails.

CHAPTER 03

What we would actually make

A three part story about money that crosses borders, and the people who send it. Built to end in a signup or a partner enquiry, whichever you want more of.

  1. A podcast episode with you or with one of your customers. Long form and honest, the kind of conversation that makes someone trust a company before they download it.
  2. Three short pieces cut from that episode. Vertical video plus written story, all one narrative, so the message does not scatter across platforms.
  3. One piece aimed at businesses instead of consumers. A case study style post about what changes when a company plugs into your rails.
  4. Distribution to my audience and into the business groups I am part of, with a tracked link so we both see what happened.
  5. A short report at the end. Reach, engagement, signups or enquiries, and what I would do differently next time.
