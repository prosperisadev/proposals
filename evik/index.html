CHAPTER 01

The problem you are actually solving

Every AI company says it has agents. Very few can show an operator why it is safe to let those agents touch their business.

01

Trust is the bottleneck, not features

A business owner is not worried about whether the agents can do the work. They are worried about who stops the agent when it does something wrong. Your answer to that is your whole advantage.

02

You are launching a category, not a product

Nobody is shopping for an AI workforce ecosystem yet. That means you explain the category first, and explaining a new category badly is slow and expensive.

03

Your buyer is not on tech Twitter

The people who sign are operators and founders. They buy from someone who sounds like they have run something, not from a thread full of rocket emojis.

CHAPTER 03

What we would actually make

A launch story for the early access phase. Explain the category in plain language, show the governance, and turn attention into waitlist signups you can count.

  1. One podcast episode with you. Why fragmented operations break growing businesses, and what actually replaces them. This is where the category gets explained properly.
  2. Three explainer pieces, vertical video plus written, each one taking a single agent and showing it doing a real job for a real business.
  3. A waitlist link tracked per piece, so after two weeks we know which story converts and which one was just nice.
  4. Distribution into my operator and small business communities, where your buyers already are.
  5. A report at the end. Signups by piece, the two angles I would double down on, and the one I would drop.
